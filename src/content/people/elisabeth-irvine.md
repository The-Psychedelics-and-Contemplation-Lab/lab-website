Elisabeth is an MSc student in Mental Health at McGill University under the supervision of Dr. Kyle Greenway. Her research focuses on improving how psychedelic therapies are studied, with particular interests in data standardization and research methodology. She coordinates the Standardizing Measures in Psychedelic Science (StaMPS) initiative, an international expert-consensus project developing a framework to guide data collection across psychedelic research and care. More broadly, she is interested in how social and professional contexts shape what researchers attend to and prioritize. Her work also extends to contemplative science, including research examining meditation-related difficulties and their interpersonal and contextual dimensions. Elisabeth plans to pursue a PhD in Clinical Psychology.
