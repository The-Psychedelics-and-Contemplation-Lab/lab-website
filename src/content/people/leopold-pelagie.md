Léopold is a PhD student in McGill’s Integrated Program in Neuroscience. Drawing on his background as a professional musician, his research examines the role of music in psychedelic therapy and how music-based approaches compare with guided meditation. His work explores how these different forms of support can shape the psychedelic experience and its therapeutic effects.
