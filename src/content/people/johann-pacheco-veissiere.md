Johann is an M.Sc. student in the Integrated Program in Neuroscience at McGill, under the supervision of Dr. Michael Lifshitz. His research combines neuroscientific and anthropological methods to collect brain and phenomenological data. He conducts fieldwork at meditation retreats and centres collecting hyper-scanning data of expert meditators and meditation teachers.
