Alyssa recently completed her MSc in Mental Health in the Department of Psychiatry at McGill University under the supervision of Dr. Kyle Greenway. Her master’s research focused on developing and validating novel observer-rated measures of trait absorption and mystical experience in ketamine-assisted psychotherapy and was supported by a SSHRC Canada Graduate Scholarship – Master’s (CGS-M). Alyssa’s broader interests lie in clinical health psychology, including psychotherapy processes and outcomes, behavioural medicine, stress and psychophysiology, and psycho-oncology. She hopes to pursue a PhD in Clinical Psychology to investigate factors that shape mental and physical health and treatment outcomes.
