Lena completed her PhD in the Integrated Program in Neuroscience at McGill University, co-supervised by Dr. Michael Lifshitz and Dr. Guillaume Dumas (Université de Montréal). A speech and language pathologist by training, with a BSc from the University of Potsdam and an MSc in Brain and Cognitive Sciences from the University of Amsterdam, her PhD research examined how interpersonal dynamics shape clinical encounters, studying clinician-patient relationships in psychedelic therapy and family dynamics in systemic family therapy through biobehavioural synchrony measures such as dual ECG, EEG hyperscanning, and movement and linguistic analysis. Her work was funded by the German Academic Scholarship Foundation, McGill’s Healthy Brains Healthy Lives initiative, the Mindstrong campaign of the Jewish General Hospital, and the Usona Institute.
