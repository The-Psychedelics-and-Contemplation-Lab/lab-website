Joe is a postdoctoral researcher in McGill’s Department of Psychiatry under the supervision of Dr. Kyle Greenway. He completed his PhD in Clinical Psychology at Duke University in 2026, including a predoctoral internship at NewYork-Presbyterian/Weill Cornell Medical Center. His research examines processes of change in psychedelic-assisted psychotherapy and mindfulness-based interventions, using methods including ecological momentary assessment (EMA) and EEG to study mind-wandering, reward processing, and therapeutic alliance. Clinically, Joe has trained in evidence-based treatments for mood, anxiety, and personality disorders across cognitive-behavioural and psychodynamic approaches. Prior to his doctoral training, he completed degrees in Psychology and Contemplative Studies at Brown University.
